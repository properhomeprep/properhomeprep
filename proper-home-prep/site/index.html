Questions
Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

FAQ
Common questions answered
How do I get started? +
Fill out our quote request form — it takes about 3 minutes. Brittany will reach out within 24 hours to discuss your property, pricing, and get you scheduled. From there, you'll complete our host intake form and we'll build your custom SOP before the first visit.
Do you provide your own cleaning supplies? +
Yes — we bring our own professional-grade cleaning supplies to every job. If you have specific product preferences (fragrance-free, specific brands, no bleach, etc.) just let us know during onboarding and we'll note it in your property SOP.
How do you handle property access? +
We work with whatever access method you have in place — lockbox, smart lock, key with Brittany, or key on site. Access codes are stored securely and shared only with the assigned tech on job day. Your access information is never distributed or stored insecurely.
What happens if damage is found during a turnover? +
Our tech documents it immediately with photos and a written damage report before cleaning around it. You're notified right away — not after the job is finished. We do not clean around or conceal damage. Every report is filed in our system and shared with you with a reference number.
What is an SOP and why does it matter? +
SOP stands for Standard Operating Procedure — it's a property-specific guide that tells our team exactly how your home should be cleaned, staged, and reset. It covers everything from how the throw pillows should be arranged to which cleaning products to avoid. It's what makes every visit consistent, even with different techs.
What is your cancellation policy? +
Cancellations with 24+ hours notice: no charge. Cancellations with less than 24 hours notice: 50% of the service fee. Same-day cancellations or no-shows (tech arrives and cannot access property): full service fee applies. We understand things come up — please just give us as much notice as possible.
Are you insured? +
Yes. Proper Home Prep carries general liability insurance. A certificate of insurance is available upon request. All team members operate under a signed confidentiality agreement.
Do you offer same-day or rush service? +
Same-day availability is not guaranteed but we do our best to accommodate urgent requests based on team availability. Rush jobs may carry an additional fee. Reach out to Brittany directly via text for fastest response on time-sensitive requests.
