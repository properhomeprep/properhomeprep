01
Arrival Protocol — Every Job, Every Time
📲 The dashboard is your system of record for every job — clock in, photos, task check-offs, and flags all happen there. Text or call Brittany only as a fallback: the dashboard is down, or you have a genuine emergency (see Emergency Contacts above).
1
Confirm the Job
Check your assignment in the dashboard. Verify property address, checkout time, and check-in window before leaving home.
2
Clock In on the Dashboard
Mark the job STARTED the moment you arrive. Dashboard down? Text Brittany "Arrived" with the property name and time instead.
3
Initial Walkthrough — Damage Check First
Walk every room BEFORE cleaning. Note and photograph anything damaged, missing, or unusual. Do not clean around damage — document it first.
4
Report Any Damage Immediately
Flag it in the dashboard — photo + description, choose severity. Do not wait until the end. Dashboard down or it's serious? Call or text Brittany immediately instead.
5
Work the SOP — Room by Room
Follow the property SOP exactly. If no SOP is on file, follow the standard turnover checklist below.
6
Final Walkthrough + Photo Check
Walk every room again before leaving. Take 3–5 completion photos: beds made, kitchen clear, bathrooms staged, front door locked.
7
Submit Sign-Off & Clock Out on the Dashboard
Mark every task complete, then submit sign-off — this sends the host their guest-ready notification. Dashboard down? Text Brittany "Complete" with a timestamp instead.

03
Proper Home Prep Quality Standards
🛏️ Beds
  • Fresh linens every turnover — no exceptions
  • Hospital corners or tucked tight
  • Decorative pillows placed per SOP photo
  • No wrinkles visible on bedspread
  • Check under beds — guests leave things
🚿 Bathrooms
  • Toilet: under rim, seat hinge, base — all sanitized
  • Mirror: streak-free, check at an angle
  • Shower: no hair, no soap scum, drain clear
  • Towels: folded hotel-style or per SOP
  • TP: at least 2 rolls visible per bathroom
🍳 Kitchen
  • Dishwasher: run → unload → reset before leaving
  • Stovetop: all burners wiped, drip pans clean
  • Inside microwave: wiped clean
  • Counters: cleared, sanitized, dry
  • Fridge: check for guest-left food — remove it
🛋️ Living Areas
  • Furniture in original layout — check SOP photo
  • Cushions/throw pillows fluffed and placed neatly
  • Remotes: present, batteries working
  • No guest belongings left behind
  • Windows, blinds, curtains — straightened
04
Do's & Don'ts

✅ Always Do

  • Clock in and submit sign-off in the dashboard for every job
  • Document any damage with photos before cleaning
  • Follow the property SOP exactly
  • Leave every light off and door locked
  • Bring your full supply kit to every job
  • Report anything that looks off — better safe than sorry
  • Stage the property exactly as shown in SOP photos
  • Double check the lockbox code is reset before you leave

🚫 Never Do

  • Never share property access codes with anyone
  • Never clean around damage — document it first
  • Never throw away items left by guests — bag & leave
  • Never use bleach unless specified in the SOP
  • Never leave a job without texting Brittany complete
  • Never move furniture that's not in your SOP
  • Never post photos of client properties on social media
  • Never give anyone your login credentials
05
Damage Reporting — When & How
🚨 Report ALL damage immediately — do not clean around it, do not wait until after the job.
📱 Step 1: Flag in the Dashboard

Photo + description, right on the job. Choose severity: Routine Note, Needs Host Attention, or Urgent — Stop and Call. Do this the moment you find it.

📸 Step 2: Photo Log

Take 3 photos minimum: wide (room context), mid (damage area), close-up (detail). All attach to the flag automatically.

🚨 Fallback: Call or Text Brittany

Dashboard down, or the damage is serious enough to select "Urgent"? Call or text Brittany immediately — don't wait on the dashboard alone.

SituationWhat To Do
Broken item (lamp, fixture, furniture)Photograph, bag any shards safely, do not discard. Flag it in the dashboard.
Stain (carpet, upholstery, mattress)Do not attempt to clean without instructions. Photograph and flag it first.
Missing item (remote, decor, appliance)Check everywhere — under beds, in closets. If truly missing, flag it.
Safety issue (exposed wire, water damage, mold)Do not continue working. Call Brittany immediately. Leave the area.
Guest belongings left behindBag and label. Do not throw away. Leave in a designated spot per SOP. Flag it in the dashboard.

07
Service-Specific Quick Notes
🔍 Vacant Property Inspections
  • Take a photo in every room — front to back, floor to ceiling
  • Check all appliances: running water, HVAC, lights
  • Check exterior: windows, doors, roof visible issues
  • Log any new issues vs. previous inspection
  • Email photo report to Brittany same day
🔧 Light Repairs & Touch-Ups
  • Photograph before starting any repair work
  • Confirm scope with Brittany before beginning
  • Do not exceed your assigned scope — call if unsure
  • Photograph after completion
  • Log all materials used for reimbursement
🌿 Exterior Refresh & Curb Appeal
  • Pressure wash: use correct PSI per surface type
  • Protect nearby vehicles and landscaping
  • Stage porch/patio exactly per SOP photo
  • Bag and dispose of all debris before leaving
  • Photo: before and after, from the street
📦 Move-In / Move-Out Prep
  • Deep clean every surface including inside cabinets
  • Check all fixtures: lights, faucets, door hardware
  • Note any damage on the walkthrough checklist
  • Confirm trash/junk removal is complete before you start
  • Leave a completion note for Brittany and the client
