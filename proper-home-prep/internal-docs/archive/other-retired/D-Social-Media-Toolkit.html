Posting Tips

  • Optimal posting times: For Nashville audiences, aim for Tuesday–Thursday between 10 AM–1 PM CDT for feed posts, and 7–9 PM CDT for Stories. Monday and Friday posts work well at 11 AM.

  • Boosting budget: Start with $15–25/day for boosted Facebook ads. Target Nashville metro, ages 28–55, interests: Airbnb hosting, property management, real estate investing. Increase spend on top performers after Week 2.

  • Story vs. Feed: Feed posts build authority and discoverability. Stories create urgency and personal connection. Use Stories for reshares, behind-the-scenes, and countdowns — always pair with a feed post earlier in the week.

  • Engagement protocol: Respond to every comment and DM within 2 hours during business hours. On Nextdoor, reply personally as Brittany — keep the tone warm, local, and non-salesy. Community trust compounds fast here.

  • Visual consistency: Stick to the brand palette — forest green, warm golds, cream tones, natural light photography. Avoid stock photos with visible watermarks. Flat-lay shots, clean spaces, and authentic team moments outperform polished studio content for service businesses.
