My Standard Operating Procedures

Access Info

Lock Type: Smart Lock (August)

Access Code: Last 4 digits of guest confirmation # (provided per booking)

Parking: Dedicated spot #4 in rear lot, accessible via alley

Notes: Ring doorbell — do not disable. Gate code for pool area: 1234#

Cleaning Standards

Standard: Full turnover clean between every guest stay

Kitchen: All dishes washed/put away, counters sanitized, appliances wiped (inside microwave + oven check)

Bathrooms: Scrub tub/shower, sanitize toilet, restock toiletries, mirrors streak-free

Floors: Vacuum all carpet, mop hardwood/tile, spot-check under furniture

Staging: Fan on low in living room, lights off, thermostat at 72°F, blinds half-open

Linen Preferences

Beds: White duvet covers, 2 pillows per guest + 1 decorative throw pillow

Towels: 2 bath towels + 1 hand towel + 1 washcloth per guest — rolled, not folded

Extras: Extra blanket folded at foot of each bed

Laundry: On-site washer/dryer. Wash on hot, dry on medium. Use unscented detergent only.

Restocking Notes

Toiletries: Refill wall-mounted dispensers (shampoo, conditioner, body wash) — brand: Public Goods

Kitchen: Dish soap, sponge, paper towels (1 roll on holder + 1 backup under sink), trash bags

Coffee: 12 K-cups (variety pack), 2 sweetener packets, 4 creamers in mini fridge

Supplies Closet: Hall closet, top shelf — backup supplies stored there

Special Instructions

Welcome Card: Place on kitchen counter — cards are in drawer next to fridge

Pet Policy: Property is pet-friendly. After pet stays, do extra lint roll on furniture + check for accidents

Checkout Photos: Take 5 photos (kitchen, living, both bedrooms, bathroom) and text to coordinator after each clean

Damage Report: Note and photograph any damage immediately. Do not attempt to fix — notify coordinator.
