04 Payment Terms

Invoicing: Invoices are issued following each completed service visit, or on a recurring schedule as agreed. Payment is due within 5 business days of invoice date unless otherwise specified in writing.

Accepted Payment Methods: Venmo, Zelle, cash, check, or credit card (processing fee may apply).

Late Payment: Invoices unpaid after 10 business days are subject to a $25 late fee plus $10 per additional 7-day period. Proper Home Prep reserves the right to suspend service on accounts with outstanding balances.

Returned Checks: A $35 returned check fee applies to any check returned for insufficient funds.

💡 For STR clients: Invoices are typically sent within 24 hours of each completed turnover. Setting up automatic payment is strongly recommended.

Scheduling: Client is responsible for notifying Proper Home Prep of upcoming service needs with reasonable advance notice (minimum 24 hours for standard services; same-day availability is not guaranteed). STR clients are encouraged to share their iCal link for automated scheduling.

06 Damage Policy & Liability

Pre-Existing Damage: Proper Home Prep performs a walkthrough and documents the property condition upon arrival. Any damage discovered is photographed and reported immediately. Proper Home Prep is not responsible for pre-existing damage.

Accidental Damage: If damage is caused by Proper Home Prep staff during service, Client will be notified promptly. Claims must be submitted in writing within 48 hours of the completed service date. Proper Home Prep's liability for any single incident is limited to the cost of the service performed on that date.

Breakage: Proper Home Prep takes care with all items in the property. Items of extraordinary value (collectibles, art, antiques) should be noted in the property SOP. Proper Home Prep is not liable for breakage of fragile items not disclosed in the SOP.

Keys & Access Devices: Client assumes all costs associated with re-keying or replacing locks in the event of a lost key.

⚠️ Proper Home Prep carries general liability insurance. A certificate of insurance is available upon request.
Client initials confirm they have read and understood the damage and liability terms above.
07 Cancellation Policy

By Client:

  • Cancellations with 24+ hours notice: No charge.
  • Cancellations with less than 24 hours notice: 50% of the scheduled service fee is due.
  • Same-day cancellations or no-shows (tech arrives and cannot access property): Full service fee is due.

By Proper Home Prep: In the event of emergency or staffing issue, Proper Home Prep will provide as much notice as possible and will not charge a cancellation fee. Rescheduling will be offered at the next available date.

Termination of Agreement: Either party may terminate this agreement with 14 days written notice. Outstanding invoices remain due upon termination. Proper Home Prep reserves the right to terminate immediately if the property poses safety risks to staff.
