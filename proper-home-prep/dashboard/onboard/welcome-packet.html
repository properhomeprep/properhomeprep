Our Services

What We Do — Every Time We Visit

Every Proper Home Prep service follows the same documented standard. No guesswork, no variability — just consistent, hospitality-grade results on every property, every visit.

01

Rental Make-Ready & STR Turnover

Our core service — complete turnover preparation between guests. Cleaning, linen setup, restocking, staging, and photo documentation, all completed within your window. Your property is guest-ready before your next check-in, every time.

02

Home Prep Cleaning

Deep, thorough cleaning that prepares any property for immediate use — whether for a new guest, a showing, or a fresh start. Every surface, every corner, cleaned to a five-star hotel standard.

03

Vacant Property Inspections

Systematic inspection and condition reporting for unoccupied properties. Every room documented with timestamped photos — so you always know exactly what's happening at your property, even when you're not there.

04

Light Repairs & Touch-Ups

Minor repairs, paint touch-ups, and punch-list completion. We handle the small things that make a big difference — so your property is truly guest-ready, not just clean.

05

Exterior Refresh & Curb Appeal

Because the guest experience starts before they open the door. Exterior cleaning, tidying, and first-impression enhancement — so every arrival feels like a five-star welcome.

06

Move-In / Move-Out Prep & Junk Removal

Full transition cleaning for residents and new occupants, plus complete cleanout services for estates, vacancies, and repositioning properties.

The Proper Promise

Every job is photo documented. Every checklist is completed in real time. Every issue is flagged before your next guest arrives. If it's not in the dashboard, it didn't happen.

Your Host Dashboard

Real-Time Visibility Into Every Turnover

The Proper Dashboard gives you live visibility into every job — from the moment your technician arrives to the moment your property is guest-ready. Here's how it works.

1

You Receive Your Welcome Packet & Onboarding Code

After signing your service agreement, you'll receive this packet along with a unique onboarding code (e.g. PHP-A3K9) and a link to your host portal. Your code expires in 30 days.

📧 Delivered via email
2

Complete Your Property SOP Online

Visit your host portal and complete your Property Standard Operating Procedure — room layout, linen preferences, restocking lists, access info, and scheduling preferences. This becomes the master profile for every technician assigned to your property.

🔗 dashboard.properhomeprep.com/onboard
3

Your Property Goes Live

Our team reviews your SOP, confirms your property profile, and activates your account. You'll receive a confirmation that your property is live and ready to schedule.

✅ Typically within 1 business day
4

Watch Your First Turnover in Real Time

Log into your host dashboard and watch as your technician checks in, uploads before photos, completes each task, and flags any issues — all in real time. When the job is complete, you receive an instant notification that your property is guest-ready.

📱 Works on any device
Live Photo Stream

Photos appear in your dashboard as they're uploaded — organized by room, timestamped, and permanently linked to the job record.

Instant Issue Flags

Any damage, missing items, or maintenance concerns are flagged immediately with photos — giving you a recovery window before your next guest arrives.

Real-Time Task Feed

Watch each task get checked off as it's completed — "Kitchen counters ✓ 2:34 PM" — so you always know exactly where your turnover stands.

Guest-Ready Notification

The moment your technician signs off, you receive an instant push notification confirming your property is complete and guest-ready.

Getting Started

Your Onboarding Checklist

Complete these steps to get your property live in the Proper Home Prep system. Most hosts are fully onboarded within 48 hours.

Step 1

Review & Sign

Read and sign your Proper Home Prep Service Agreement
Review this Welcome Packet in full
Save your onboarding code: PHP-____ (provided separately)
Step 2

Complete Your Property SOP

Visit your onboarding portal and enter your code
Complete Section A — Property Profile & Access Information
Complete Section B — Room Layout & Linen Inventory
Complete Section C — Cleaning Standards & Preferences
Complete Section D — Restocking & Amenities
Complete Sections E–I — Damage, Scheduling, Special Instructions, Sign-Off
Upload any staging reference photos to your property profile
Step 3

Set Up Your Dashboard

Log into your host dashboard at dashboard.properhomeprep.com
Set your notification preferences (email / SMS / push)
Connect your calendar (Airbnb / VRBO / iCal) for automatic scheduling
Add a billing method to your account
Step 4

Schedule Your First Service

Contact your coordinator to schedule your first turnover or walkthrough
Confirm access method and entry code with your coordinator
Watch your first job live in the dashboard — and enjoy the peace of mind
